Style/medium: premium cinematic sci-fi concept art, realistic materials, mature game UI atmosphere rather than a poster
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape, central 55% kept dark and visually quiet for readable chat content, architectural detail concentrated at the far edges and lower corners, strong depth through the viewport
Lighting/mood: low-key navy-black cockpit, cool cyan instrument glow, sparse disciplined red accents, calm high-stakes command atmosphere
Color palette: graphite black, deep navy, desaturated cobalt, ice cyan, N7-like signal red used sparingly
Materials/textures: matte armored metal, smoked glass, faint brushed alloy, subtle volumetric light
Constraints: original artwork; no copied game screenshot; no characters; no readable text; no logos; no watermark; no large spaceship in the center; preserve ample dark negative space for UI readability
Avoid: neon overload, generic cyberpunk city, purple AI gradient, busy HUD overlays, lens flare across the center, decorative particles covering the chat area
